Sell from one stock in your Square shop and your Swell store, and keep every till sale in your Swell order history. When something sells, arrives or is recounted on either side, the other side has it, usually within a minute. Each sale completed in Square becomes a paid order in Swell, with the lines and total of its receipt, to the cent.

Connect Square with an access token from your own Square developer application. The app runs inside Swell, with no other service in between, and leaves your Square catalog, orders and customers as they are: in Square it only adjusts stock.

- **Sales on both sides count.** The app passes on what changed, never a total that overwrites the other side. A sale at the till and an online order for the same item at the same moment both come off the stock, in Square and in Swell.
- **Linked by SKU, started by you.** Match products pairs each Square item with the Swell product or variant that has the same SKU, and lists both quantities. You start the pairs and say which side has the right numbers. Items with a doubled or missing SKU, or without stock tracking, stay unlinked, with the reason.
- **A stock history you can read.** Every change from Square appears in the product's stock history in Swell, marked Square POS, with what happened: sold, received, recounted. Online orders arrive in Square as stock sold.
- **Till sales as paid orders.** Lines, discounts, taxes, tips and the total are those of the receipt. The orders are numbered SQ- with Square's receipt number, are paid with the Square POS payment method, need no fulfillment, take no stock a second time and send your customers no e-mail.
- **On the customer's account.** A sale with a Square customer goes to the Swell account with the same e-mail address. The app creates no customers: all other sales go to one walk-in account.
- **Refunds follow.** A refund made in Square is recorded on its order, which then shows as partly refunded or refunded.
- **Nothing guessed, nothing lost.** Every five minutes the app compares all linked items, even when Square sent no notice. What can't be synced or imported is listed under Needs attention with the reason, and your administrators get one e-mail. Once the cause is fixed, retry the sale or start the item again. Nothing is imported twice.

Setup takes a few minutes. In the Square Developer Console, create an application and copy its production access token. In Swell, paste the token in the app settings and run Test connection. Turn on the stock sync, choose Match products, and start the matched items under Products > Square stock. Then turn on the sales import; earlier sales can be imported from a date you choose. To try the app first, use your application's sandbox token in your store's test environment.

Stock is shared with one Square location, and sales can be imported from all of them. Products are not created or changed on either side, and items sold by weight are not linked. Imported orders carry the receipt's lines but no products, so Swell's sales reports by product leave out till sales.
